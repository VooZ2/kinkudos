# Parent settings

Path: **Parents → Settings**. Start with the family rules; leave the administrator-only areas unchanged unless you understand their effect.

## Family rules

You can change the family name, photo bonus, birthday points, lottery switch, ticket price, weekly ticket limit, and image-retention periods. These settings affect future activity, not completed point entries or purchased tickets.

- Set a photo or birthday value to `0` to disable that bonus.
- Disabling lottery tickets stops new purchases and reminders; an open ticket can still be completed.
- Pending task photos, revision-requested task photos, and unresolved feedback screenshots are not removed automatically.

## Other settings areas

- **Child devices:** pair, rename, or revoke a device.
- **Family accounts and application settings:** create, edit, or deactivate parent accounts and child profiles. Earlier history stays preserved.
- **Family feedback:** filter in-app reports, view an attached screenshot, and save the review status.

Only the family administrator can change network access, email settings, backup settings, or revoke all child devices.
