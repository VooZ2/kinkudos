# Rewards, goals, and lottery tickets

Create rewards in **Parents → Tasks and rewards**. A child requests a reward; the request stays pending until a parent approves or rejects it. Approval checks the child's allowed balance floor.

Children can also propose a new reward or a personal savings goal. Parents set the final point cost when approving a proposal. A savings goal shows progress against the child's overall balance; it does not reserve points separately.

Lottery tickets are optional. The family setting controls whether they are available at all; a child can also have lottery access turned off individually. The ticket price and weekly limit apply to future purchases, and the limit resets every Monday for each child.
