# Child space

Children see their own tasks, rewards, goals, recent point history, and profile settings. They cannot see another child’s private information, PIN, balance, or requests.

## What a child can do

| Area | What the child can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Tasks** | Choose an available task, optionally attach a photo, and submit it for parent approval. If work is returned for improvement, revise it and submit it again. |
| **Today’s assigned tasks** | Complete tasks sent by a parent. Each completion awards its points immediately and the task disappears from today’s list. |
| **Rewards** | Request an available reward when the balance and credit rule allow it. A child may cancel their own request while it is still pending. |
| **Goals and suggestions** | Suggest a new reward or a personal savings goal. A parent decides whether to approve it and sets the final point amount. A goal tracks progress against the overall balance; points are not set aside separately. |
| **Lottery tickets** | Buy an optional ticket only when enabled for both the family and the child, the child has enough points, and the weekly limit allows it. An open ticket must be finished before buying another. |
| **Give points** | If another active child exists, send only points already earned to them. A child cannot transfer credit or points they do not have. |
| **Feedback** | Send a private idea or problem report to the parents, optionally with a screenshot. |

## Personalise the child space

At the bottom of the child dashboard, open **Settings**.

### Appearance and themes

The child can choose one of the built-in worlds: Neutral, Magic Academy, Block World, Superhero HQ, Art Studio, Panda World, or Blockville World. A theme changes the look, wording, point-unit names, and small sounds; it does not change balances, rules, or which rewards are available.

**Change my theme randomly every day** chooses a different theme automatically once each day. Turn it off to keep the selected theme.

### Avatar

The child may upload a JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, or HEIF image up to 5 MB. KinKudos crops it to a square and stores the processed avatar for the family only. Parents see the avatar on the child card.

### Birthday

The child may request a birthday change. A parent must approve it before the date changes. The birthday is used only for the optional yearly birthday-points rule.

### Change PIN

The child can change their own four-digit PIN by entering the current PIN, the new PIN, and the new PIN again. They should choose a PIN that siblings and friends do not know. A parent can also reset a child PIN from **Parents → Settings → Child profiles**.

## What still needs a parent

A child cannot create parent accounts, change family rules, approve their own task or reward, change the credit limit, pair a new device, turn on network restrictions, configure email or backups, or restore data.
