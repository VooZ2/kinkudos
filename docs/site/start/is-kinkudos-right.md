# Is KinKudos right for your family?

KinKudos works best for one family that wants a private, shared way to make
everyday responsibilities visible. Parents decide the rules; children see a
clear next step, their progress, and the rewards they are working towards.

> **For:** Parents deciding whether to use KinKudos<br>
> **You need:** A family member who can operate the server<br>
> **Result:** A clear decision before spending time on installation

## It is a good fit when…

- you want tasks, points, rewards, and decisions in one private family space;
- an adult is comfortable maintaining a small Linux/Docker installation, or
  can ask someone they trust to do that work;
- children can use a phone, tablet, or computer that the family controls; and
- you prefer to choose your own rules instead of using a service that decides
  them for you.

KinKudos supports the everyday loop: a child completes work, a parent reviews
or records it, points appear in History, and the child can request a reward or
track a goal. It also supports direct parent awards for work an adult has
already observed.

## It may not be the right fit when…

KinKudos is not a hosted account you can simply sign up for. It does not
provide a server, a domain name, email provider, remote-backup provider, or
someone who manages those things for you. There is no guaranteed support or
response time for custom hosting setups.

If no one in the family can run Docker, maintain HTTPS, apply updates, and
keep backups safe, choose a managed family tool instead or arrange help from a
trusted server administrator first.

## What KinKudos does not decide

KinKudos does not decide which chores are fair, what a reward is worth, or
whether scratch tickets belong in your family. Parents set those rules. The
optional scratch-ticket feature uses only the family’s internal points: it is not a money,
purchase, or external gambling service.

The in-app feedback form is also private to your own family installation. It
does not send a report to the KinKudos project maintainer.
